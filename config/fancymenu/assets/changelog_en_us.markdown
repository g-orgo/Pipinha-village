### Pipinha's Village
### 1.21.1 Edition
^^^

Welcome to Pipinha's Village, now on Minecraft 1.21.1! Based on Society: Sunlit Valley.

This version only exists because we wanted Create Aeronautics in the pack, and it did not exist for 1.20.1.

### New
+ Create Aeronautics: physics-based contraptions, from cars and boats to planes and airships (Simulated, Aeronautics and Offroad)
+ Aeronautics Camera Sync: the camera follows the aircraft you are riding
+ Create High Seas: physics contraptions float on water and can flood inside
+ Sable: moving block structures with physics
+ Let it Walk!: walking machines for Create, with legs driven by Sable physics, plus a remote control to pilot them from afar
+ Create Waystones Integration: waystones ride along on Create contraptions and work again wherever they stop
+ FTB Stuff & Things: hammers, sluices, cobblestone generators, compressed blocks and other small machines
+ Create 6 with its addons updated
+ Botania Revived replaces Botania: the same experience as 1.20.1, with the seeds recreated
+ Sodium + Iris instead of Embeddium + Oculus: shaders and performance on the new version
+ Free look: hold left Alt while mounted on a horse or another mount to look around without turning the character
+ Auto Hide HUD: the interface hides itself when it is not in use
+ Clothing from any mod goes in the Curios head, body and feet slots
+ Lanterns and hot or cold items count in either hand and also in Curios
+ Hostile mobs only spawn below level 50 or in other dimensions, so the overworld surface is calmer
+ Towns with the classic street layout from 1.20.1
+ Tall Dramatic Doors complete in NPC houses and blueprints

### Changed
+ Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.265
+ Some key binds were renamed (ParCool, Refined Storage, Relics and Punchy): set them again in Controls if needed
+ Worlds from the 1.20.1 edition are converted on a copy, and blocks from mods that did not come along disappear

### Removed
- Splendid Slimes and Create: Splendid Cogs, along with their quests, offers and recipes
- Mods without a 1.21.1 version, such as Species, Quark Delight and Refined Storage Addons

### In progress
+ Shops, professions, economy, eating animations and quests from the 1.20.1 edition are being brought over step by step

Questions? Open the Welcome chapter in the quest book, the Guides are there.
